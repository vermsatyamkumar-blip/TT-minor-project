🔍 What the Metrics Mean

R² Score ({{ "%.2f"|format(metrics.r2 * 100) }}%)

The model explains {{ "%.1f"|format(metrics.r2 * 100) }}% of the variance in final scores. A perfect model has R²=100%. This is a strong fit.

MAE ({{ metrics.mae }})

On average, the predictions are off by {{ metrics.mae }} marks. This means most predictions are within ~4 marks of the actual score.

RMSE ({{ metrics.rmse }})

Penalizes larger errors more than MAE. An RMSE of {{ metrics.rmse }} indicates the model rarely makes large mistakes.
